# Design notes

HossRide, Hossein Tabasi, 2026. Companion to `master-prompt.html`.
These notes record why the specification looks and is numbered as it is.
They are not a second specification.

## Palette

The ground is wet asphalt, near-black. Street light is sodium-amber.
Emphasis (pull quotes, coal) is ember red. The three roles must remain
distinct. Sodium is illumination. Ember is heat. Asphalt is the unlit
mass that both of those colours fall on.

Hex approximations live in `PALETTE.md`. They are tokens for the
specification documents and for a later material pass. They are not
laboratory readings.

Night is permanent. A light theme on the HTML page is a reading aid. It
is not a climate in the game.

## Type

Headings use Oswald, condensed, in the register of highway signage.
Body uses IBM Plex Sans. Data, tokens, and section numbers as figures
use IBM Plex Mono.

The HTML page loads those faces from Google Fonts. Other files in this
repository do not. Print and offline reading fall back to a condensed
sans, a text sans, and a monospace.

Do not use a display face that suggests daylight tourism. The type
should read as night instruction.

## Why the numbering is dependency order

The fourteen sections are numbered by what must exist before the next
directive can be obeyed.

- 01 and 02 state the premise and the governor. Filtration is prior to
  every downstream call. A builder who starts at traffic or audio will
  write the wrong loop: update by speed, then cull.
- 03 is the treadmill. Vehicles, rain, smoke, and the tower all move in
  a world whose origin is the immobilised rider. Without 03 there is no
  legal motion quantity.
- 04 admits traffic into that world. Thirteen is a closed count. Lofted
  profile is the construction method. 42 percent white paint is a
  population parameter. None of those facts can be implemented until
  relative velocity exists.
- 05, 06, and 07 are the machine, the rider, and the cigarette. They
  are three smoke jobs. They require bodies in the treadmill frame.
  They cannot share an emitter, for reasons of lifetime, lighting, and
  budget given in the specification.
- 08 locks climate. 09 places lightning on a dedicated light and locks
  Milad Tower geometry. Lightning and the tower need a night, wet
  world that already moves.
- 10 is audio after the mix targets exist: engine from the machine,
  rain from climate. The mix is lopsided by design.
- 11 and 12 refuse a collectible economy and close the ride at
  Amirabad. A close cannot be specified before there is a ride.
- 13 is the Android ship. 14 is the audit. Both come last because they
  test the whole.

Reorder the sections and the specification becomes a brochure. Keep the
order and it remains an instrument.

## Treadmill argument

An endless rider appears to travel. If the rider's world position is
integrated, that position grows without bound. Particles, voices,
streaming segments, and collision then chase a moving origin. Error
grows with distance. Filtration becomes a function of two large
numbers.

Immobilise the rider. Hold the camera with the rider. Move the world.
Infinite distance is then a recycling of world content, not a growing
coordinate. Relative velocity is a single quantity: the velocity of
admitted content with respect to a fixed origin.

The two theses lock together. Filtration decides which objects are
live at that origin. Relative velocity is the only motion those objects
may use. The rider never accumulates a world position. Amirabad
approaches. The rider does not depart.
