HossRide
Tehran, After Dark

A single-document reconstruction of every system, decision and constraint that constitutes HossRide — an endless motorcycle traversal of a perpetually rain-slicked Tehran expressway, rendered in Godot 4 and shipped as a signed Android package. What follows is the specification in full: not a summary of the artefact, but the instrument capable of producing it again.

01

Premise

Construct an endless-runner in the third person, in which the player pilots a large-displacement cruiser motorcycle through dense, adversarial traffic on a multi-lane Tehran expressway. The fantasy under construction is not velocity in the abstract — it is filtration: the specific, disreputable pleasure of threading a motorcycle through interstitial gaps that were never intended to admit one.

This single premise governs every downstream decision. Lateral position must therefore be continuous rather than lane-quantised; the discretised three-lane hop of the genre's canonical exemplars is expressly rejected, since the gaps worth exploiting are not themselves lane-shaped. Lane geometry remains consequential, but it is the property of the traffic, never of the protagonist.

The register is atmospheric rather than arcade. It is always night. It is always raining. A cigarette burns for the entire duration of the ride. The destination is not a score but a specific house, in a specific district, where specific people are waiting.

Weaving proximate to traffic accrues a multiplier that decays continuously. A cautious rider still earns; a reckless one earns several times more. The economy is therefore an argument about how to ride, expressed in arithmetic.
02

Technical Foundation

Implement in Godot 4.7 using GDScript throughout, under the mobile rendering method — Forward+ is architecturally superior on desktop but prohibitively expensive on the target hardware, and the mobile backend retains the physically-based shading, real-time shadowing and glow post-processing on which the night presentation wholly depends.

Adopt a strict posture of procedural generation for all geometry. Not a single mesh is imported. Every vehicle, every structure, the motorcycle, the rider and the landmark tower are constructed at runtime from parameterised descriptions. This is a deliberate concession to the absence of an art pipeline, and it is architected so that authored assets may later displace the procedural equivalents without disturbing the surrounding systems.

03

The Treadmill

Resolve the infinite-world problem by immobilising the protagonist. The rider is pinned permanently at the world origin on the longitudinal axis; the world is translated past him. This confers two decisive advantages: floating-point precision remains pristine across arbitrary distance, and relative velocity becomes the sole quantity of consequence.

A traffic vehicle's apparent motion reduces to its speed − the rider's speed. Overtaking, being overtaken, and closing on a labouring bus all emerge from that single expression. Nothing further is required.

The carriageway is a recirculating ring of eight chunks of sixty metres, four lanes at 3.5 m. When a chunk falls behind the camera it is translated to the far end of the ring and its roadside furniture re-randomised. Nothing is instantiated or freed during play; frame time consequently remains flat indefinitely.

Boost Pads

Distribute glowing chevron strips across the tarmac — one candidate per chunk, live on roughly 55% of them, in a randomised lane. Render them unshaded and emissive, so that they read identically at any hour: a reward invisible in darkness is a trap rather than a reward. Traversal confers +15 m/s of ceiling plus an immediate impulse, bleeding away across approximately four seconds.

04

Tehran Traffic

This is the system upon which the location's credibility rests entirely. Populate the carriageway with the vehicles that genuinely constitute Iranian road traffic, and describe each not as a box but as two lofted cross-sectional profiles: a lower body from sill to beltline, and a greenhouse above it.

Each profile is a sequence of rings; each ring is a rounded rectangle rather than a hard rectangle. This last detail is disproportionately load-bearing. Real automotive bodies possess radiused shoulders and crowned roofs, and a boxed silhouette will never resolve into a car irrespective of how accomplished the lighting becomes.

The consequence is structural differentiation rather than recolouring: a Paykan emerges boxy and upright with near-vertical glass, inherited from its 1960s Hillman Hunter lineage; a 206 emerges short and round with a steeply truncated hatch; a Samand emerges tall and softened on its 405 platform.

Verisimilitude in the Aggregate

Weight the paint distribution as it genuinely falls: 42% white, 18% silver, 12% black, with saturated colours comparatively scarce. Admit yellow and green taxi liveries with roof signage on the appropriate bodies. Institute lane discipline — heavy vehicles gravitate to the nearside; quick hatchbacks inhabit the outer lanes.

Behaviour

Vehicles decelerate for whatever precedes them in lane, illuminating brake lamps as they do. They change lanes only where a genuine gap exists, easing toward the new centreline and rolling fractionally into the manoeuvre. They react to the horn: approximately 40% change lane, 30% merely shuffle aside within their own, and the remainder — Tehran being Tehran — disregard it entirely. Buses and trucks never yield to a motorcycle.

Pool all instances by body type. Constructing a Samand mesh is expensive; constructing it once and recirculating the instance indefinitely is not.

05

The Machine

The player begins on a large, wide, low-slung V-twin cruiser — 1.74 m wheelbase, 0.40 m wheels, a 0.68 m seat, tall risers carrying the bars high and drawn back, twin low pipes, deep valanced mudguards over both wheels and forward-set footboards. Its considerable mass renders it ponderous between lanes; that is the levy exacted for how planted and imposing it feels.

Assemble it as an articulated hierarchy rather than a monolithic mesh, since its constituents must move independently: the front assembly steers, both wheels rotate, the rider leans against the frame, and his left hand departs the bar entirely.

No component may be matte black. A motorcycle assembled uniformly in black disappears against wet nocturnal asphalt. Every component receives its own value and finish — chromed forks and bars, a gunmetal spine, polished alloy swingarm, tan leather saddle, matching brown grips — so that the machine resolves into discrete parts at a glance. Legibility constraint

Wheels

White rubber, near-white polished alloy, and a sport spoke pattern: five twin-spoke pairs, each mounted on its own pivot so that they constitute true radial arms rather than bars spanning the full diameter — the distinction between a cast alloy and a wire wheel. A dark brake disc on one side prevents the wheel resolving into a flat white circle.

Registration

A white plate bearing 4N5555/IRAN, with a blue province strip, suspended clear of the rear wheel on a visible bracket and canted back ten degrees. The chase camera occupies the space behind the machine, which makes the rear plate the single component permanently addressed to the player.

Handling Model
throttle
Automatic. The ceiling ascends with distance travelled, to +20 m/s over the base machine.
steering
Lean-derived. Lateral velocity issues from lean angle, never directly from input — this is what confers mass.
authority
Attenuates with speed. Trivially flickable at 30 km/h; requires commitment at 150.
barrier
Contact scrubs speed and rights the machine rather than terminating the run.
input
Device tilt, touch drag and keyboard, all three live simultaneously.
06

The Rider

Construct Hossein as a segmented figure, articulated at shoulder, elbow and wrist, and at hip, knee and ankle. The bends at elbow and knee are precisely what distinguish a person from a mannequin; a limb rendered as a single capsule will always read as the latter.

Build the torso from two stacked sections at differing widths so that it tapers from shoulder to waist as a jacket genuinely hangs. Broaden the chest across and flatten it front-to-back — people are not cylindrical. Append a neck, ears, hair and a short beard.

Dress him casually: an olive canvas jacket over a plain tee visible at the open collar, blue jeans, white trainers with dark soles. No helmet. The bare head resolves far more convincingly as a person.

On the cruiser he sits reclined at seventeen degrees with his hands elevated near shoulder height on the tall risers and his feet thrown forward onto the boards. The riding posture is derived from the machine's declared style, and it does more for a motorcycle's character than any quantity of bodywork.

07

The Cigarette

He smokes for the entire duration of the ride. He ignites one as the run commences and continues until he arrives. The left hand remains off the bar holding it throughout — correct for a motorcycle, since the right hand must retain the throttle — and he therefore rides one-handed from beginning to end. This costs the player nothing; steering authority is untouched. The imposition would defeat the purpose.

Every 2.6 to 4.4 seconds the arm elevates and the elbow folds to bring it to his mouth, holds for somewhat over half a second, and descends.

Three Distinct Emissions

One particle system cannot discharge all three obligations. Implement them separately: a continuous thread trailing from the ember; an exhalation, discharged as a single burst from a marker on his face roughly a third of a second after the hand descends, because one holds it in first; and an ember flare, escalating from 6× to 15× emission during the inhalation. At road speed the exhaled plume is torn rearward almost immediately, so it launches hard along the negative longitudinal axis at 6–12 m/s and expands as it disperses.

Scale the cigarette and ember well beyond veracity. A dimensionally accurate cigarette is invisible at this camera distance, and an invisible prop is not a prop.

08

Atmosphere

Pin the clock to 22:30 permanently. Rain perpetually, its intensity drifting between a steady shower and a downpour so that the weather retains movement without ever relenting. Both commence at full strength on the opening frame; the roads are wet and reflective immediately.

Drive surface wetness as a single global parameter that every cached material responds to simultaneously. Wet asphalt must both darken and sharpen its reflections — roughness collapsing from 0.88 to 0.14, with metallic ascending — because either transformation alone is unconvincing.

Lightning

Strike every 9 to 26 seconds. Execute the flash on a dedicated light rather than the sun: the sun's energy is interpolated toward its target every frame by the day-night system, and any value written to it would be obliterated before becoming perceptible. Each strike is a stuttering double flash — peak, collapse to 18%, recover to 82%, extinguish — because a single fade resolves as a lamp rather than as lightning. Thunder trails by distance: 0.35 seconds for a proximate crack, up to 6.5 for a remote one.

Fog

Keep it deliberately thin. Heavier fog is more attractive at close range, but at permanent night under permanent rain it obliterates Milad Tower at 800 metres — and the landmark holding the skyline matters more than the haze. This is an acknowledged trade.

09

The Skyline

Erect Milad Tower at full scale — a 250-metre fluted octagonal shaft on a splayed base, the signature bulging observation pod, and a 64-metre antenna spire capped with a red aircraft warning beacon. Illuminate it with vertical light strips ascending the shaft and glowing bands encircling the pod.

Position it dead centre at the terminus of the road, 960 metres distant, and never scroll it. Straight down the carriageway is the sole bearing guaranteed unobstructed: the roadside blocks wall off everything beyond fifteen metres laterally, so any off-axis placement is occluded regardless of distance. At 960 metres the tip subtends approximately thirty degrees of elevation — within the vertical half-field, with margin as the field widens under speed.

Cap roadside buildings at nine storeys, exceptionally fifteen. Most of Tehran along an expressway genuinely is mid-rise, and just as consequentially the roofline must remain beneath the tower's skyline angle or the landmark vanishes behind the nearest apartment block.

Pin the snow-capped Alborz ridge to the camera rig as a true horizon. Dispatch an airliner across the sky every 40 to 90 seconds — fuselage, swept wings, nacelles, tail fin, and the light rig that is genuinely all one perceives of an aircraft in a dark sky: steady red to port, green to starboard, a red beacon, and a white anti-collision strobe discharging a double blink every 1.4 seconds.

10

Audio Architecture

Implement a radio, not a playlist. It persists across menus, runs, crashes and the garage without ever restarting a track merely because the player died. That continuity is the entire proposition: the ride should feel as though it has a soundtrack running beneath it, not a music cue that resets. Shuffle via Fisher-Yates; derive artist and title from the filename; present a now-playing strip; duck beneath collisions.

Synthesise the entire non-musical sound set from first principles in Python with NumPy — no samples acquired, none bundled.

Synthesis Methods
engine
A combustion pulse train at firing frequency through a resonant exhaust, not stacked sinusoids — that is what yields the uneven thump of a single-cylinder. Two layers, pitch-tracked, with a simulated gearbox so revs climb, snap back and climb again.
horns
Paired detuned reeds with pronounced odd harmonics, four variants at differing intervals, plus a thinner high single reed for the rider's own.
thunder
A broadband crack over a low rumble amplitude-modulated by slow stochastic swells, so that it tumbles rather than fading — that irregularity is the whole character.
voice
Formant synthesis over Persian vowel targets with shouted pitch contours and consonantal onsets. Carries the vowel colour and syllabic rhythm of an exclamation from a car window; carries no words.
crash
Impact burst, sub thud, inharmonic metallic resonances, and gated debris scatter.

The Mix

Weight it decisively toward the music. The engine is a perpetual drone and the rain a perpetual hiss; at parity they bury the station entirely. The engine's fundamental sits at 0.30, its upper layer at 0.17, the rain bed at 0.26, all beneath a master of 0.48, while the station occupies full level. The imbalance is intentional.

11

Economy & Garage

Institute no collectibles whatsoever. Credits accrue purely by distance, at 100 per kilometre, scaled by a multiplier that ascends 0.35 per proximate pass — anything within 1.3 metres — to a ceiling of 5×, and decays continuously at 0.55 per second. Holding it demands sustained exposure.

Offer five machines and eight paints, each colour named in both English and Persian. Assign factory colours explicitly per machine rather than deriving them from a hash: a hash will eventually select black, and a black tank on a black road at night is precisely the failure being designed against.

Four upgrade tracks — engine, brakes, handling, nitro — across five levels, priced on a 1.7× geometric progression scaled to the machine's value. Present the garage as a live turntable rendering of the actual machine, lit as the world is lit, so that what is purchased is what is ridden.

12

The Homecoming

The run terminates not in failure but in arrival. At eleven kilometres — approximately five minutes of riding — he reaches Amirabad.

Construct the whole scene six hundred metres out so that it resolves from the haze rather than materialising; suspend traffic generation at that instant so the final stretch clears itself naturally. A green municipal banner spans the carriageway bearing AMIRABAD. Beyond it stands a two-storey beige-brick Tehran house — flat roof and parapet, lit windows, balcony with railing, rooftop water tank, garden wall, and its name illuminated across the roof.

A man, a woman and a child stand in the middle of the road, bouncing and waving on a loop from the moment they come into view — already celebrating as he appears, rather than triggering awkwardly upon arrival. Light them from directly overhead and again from in front, so faces and raised arms catch light rather than being lit solely from above.

At ninety-five metres he rolls off the throttle and collisions are disarmed — one cannot crash on one's own doorstep. He coasts to a halt just short of them. They cheer for 3.4 seconds. Then the summary reads YOU MADE IT HOME rather than RUN OVER.

13

Build Pipeline

Produce a signed Android package targeting arm64-v8a. Establish the entire toolchain portably — JDK 17, Android SDK command-line tools, build-tools 34, platform 34 and a debug keystore, all resident within the project tree, requiring no administrative rights and touching neither the system path nor the registry.

Enable ETC2/ASTC texture compression, without which Android export is refused outright. Generate the launcher icon set in three distinct forms, since Android's are not interchangeable: a legacy square icon, a full-bleed adaptive background blurred and darkened, and an adaptive foreground confined to the central 66% safe zone — because the launcher masks and parallaxes the layers independently, and a full-bleed foreground has its subject decapitated on most launchers. 

godot --headless --path <project> --import
godot --headless --path <project> --export-debug "Android" HossRide.apk
Anticipate one non-obvious obstruction: antivirus software performing HTTPS inspection re-signs every TLS connection with its own root certificate. Windows trusts it; the JVM maintains an entirely separate truststore that does not. The SDK manager consequently fails with PKIX path building failed and presents exactly as a blocked connection. Directing Java at the Windows certificate store resolves it. Encountered and resolved

Standing Constraints

These govern the whole and are not negotiable against convenience.

Non-Negotiable
music
Ship none. Build the station; the operator supplies recordings they hold rights to. Commercial music is neither acquired nor bundled.
vehicles
Original approximations evoking the Tehran street mix — stylised likenesses, neither licensed nor dimensionally exact.
legibility
Under permanent night, anything that must be seen must be lit, emissive, or oversized. Accuracy loses to visibility every time.
fairness
No characterful flourish may cost the player anything. The cigarette, the horn and the arrival are free.
performance
Nothing instantiated or freed mid-run. Pool everything. Share every material.
